CRICKET - BASIC INFORMATION

Cricket is a team sport played between two teams of eleven players each. The game is played on a large field with a rectangular 22-yard pitch in the center.

BASIC RULES

1. One team bats while the other team bowls and fields.
2. The batting team tries to score as many runs as possible.
3. The bowling team tries to dismiss the batters and stop them from scoring runs.
4. A run is usually scored when the two batters run between the wickets.
5. A boundary scored by hitting the ball to the edge of the field is worth 4 runs if the ball touches the ground before reaching the boundary.
6. A boundary hit directly over the boundary without touching the ground is worth 6 runs.
7. Common ways a batter can be dismissed include bowled, caught, run out, lbw, and stumped.

FORMATS OF CRICKET

Test Cricket:
Test matches are the longest format and can be played for up to five days. Each team normally gets two innings.

One Day International (ODI):
An ODI match normally has a maximum of 50 overs per team.

T20 Cricket:
T20 is a shorter format in which each team normally has a maximum of 20 overs.

IMPORTANT CRICKET TERMS

Run:
A unit used to measure the score.

Over:
Six legal deliveries bowled by one bowler.

Wicket:
It can refer to the set of three stumps and two bails, or to the dismissal of a batter.

Innings:
The period during which a team or batter is batting.

Bowler:
The player who delivers the ball to the batter.

Batter:
The player who attempts to score runs.

Fielder:
A player who tries to stop runs and take catches or make dismissals.

All-rounder:
A player who contributes significantly with both batting and bowling.

EXAMPLE

Suppose Team A scores 180 runs in 20 overs. Team B then needs 181 runs to win. If Team B scores 181 runs before losing all its wickets or using all its overs, Team B wins the match.

WHY CRICKET IS POPULAR

Cricket is popular in many countries, especially in India, Australia, England, Pakistan, South Africa, New Zealand, Sri Lanka, and Bangladesh. International cricket includes major competitions such as the Cricket World Cup and T20 World Cup.
